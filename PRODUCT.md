# Studio PRA

## Product purpose
Studio PRA, Partnership Romano & Antonazzo, is an international law firm founded in 2004. The website must convince cross-border private and business clients that the firm is a serious, reachable and internationally experienced legal partner for matters between Italy, Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

## Register
brand

## Audience
- German, Austrian and Swiss clients who need legal assistance in Italy.
- Italian clients who need legal assistance in German-speaking jurisdictions.
- Companies, investors, families and private clients facing cross-border legal matters.
- Visitors who are likely stressed, time-sensitive and evaluating trust before making first contact.

## Brand voice
Composed, authoritative, international, precise, discreetly premium.

## Strategic principles
- Lead with trust: established since 2004, multiple jurisdictions, visible offices, direct contact.
- Make the international bridge immediately clear: Italy ↔ DACH region.
- Reduce uncertainty: clear language pathways, office presence, concrete contact options.
- Premium without ostentation: legal competence, calm authority, rigorous typography, measured motion.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages.
- Shiny gradients, bouncing effects, emoji-heavy legal marketing.
- Loud luxury clichés, gold overload, stock-photo excess.
- Dense legal walls of text before the trust proposition is understood.
