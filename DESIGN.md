# Studio PRA Design System

## Direction
A premium international law-firm landing page: dark legal ink, warm ivory, restrained burgundy and antique gold accents. The design should feel like a private cross-border advisory office, not a template.

## Color tokens
Use OKLCH where possible.
- Ink: `oklch(17% 0.025 35)`
- Ink lifted: `oklch(24% 0.03 35)`
- Ivory: `oklch(96% 0.012 82)`
- Parchment: `oklch(91% 0.025 82)`
- Burgundy: `oklch(43% 0.17 28)`
- Burgundy deep: `oklch(31% 0.13 28)`
- Gold: `oklch(73% 0.105 82)`
- Muted text: `oklch(58% 0.025 55)`

## Typography
- Keep Google Fonts. Use Montserrat with strong contrast via weight, tracking and scale.
- Large display headline, composed body text, short uppercase labels only where useful.
- Avoid decorative serif defaults and generic editorial tropes.

## Layout
- Asymmetric hero: proof and contact on one side, brand statement on the other.
- Use trust bands, jurisdiction pathways and office presence as conversion elements.
- Cards are allowed only when they express distinct decisions: client direction, office, contact path.

## Motion
- Subtle reveal, hover lift, marker focus. No bounce, no elastic, no layout-property animation.

## Conversion priorities
1. Immediate clarity: Studio PRA handles Italy-DACH legal matters.
2. Trust: founded 2004, international offices, specialist positioning.
3. Action: choose correct language/site or write/call the closest office.
